# Saltmarrow story bible: places and world rules

## The city

Saltmarrow is built inside and upon the skeleton of a leviathan that beached on the Greywater
shore four hundred years ago. The ribs are the city's walls and bridges; the skull is the
harbor; the spine runs along the high ridge. There are seven tiers, counted from the water up.

## Places

- **The Gullet**: the covered market in the leviathan's throat, on the first tier. Lit by
  whale-oil lamps that burn green. Sells kelp, rope, salt and gossip.
- **Gullet Harbor**: inside the skull. Tobiah Fenn's watch-house sits in the left eye socket.
- **The Rib Bridges**: nine bridges of bone that cross the flooded channels between the ribs.
  The fourth bridge is cracked and roped off; it is where Corin Varrow drowned.
- **The Lantern Stair**: four hundred and twelve steps cut into the spine, from the Gullet to
  the Spine Chapel. Pell has counted them more than any living person.
- **The Spine Chapel**: on the seventh tier, built over the leviathan's heart. The heart is a
  room of red stone no one may enter except the Abbess.
- **Ilse's room**: above a chandlery on the third tier. One window of oiled horn, a map table
  made from a door, and a hammock instead of a bed.

## World rules (canon, never broken)

1. **The tide-bells.** The Order of the Spine rings the tide-bells three times before the Low
   Hour. During the Low Hour the channels drain and then flood back all at once. No one crosses
   the Rib Bridges during the Low Hour. Anyone caught on a bridge when the third bell stops is
   considered already dead.
2. **The Glass Edict.** No glass above the third tier. Windows up there are oiled horn or
   nothing. Glass "draws the leviathan's dreams". Lanterns on the high tiers are horn too.
3. **Salt-script.** Words written in brine on bare skin come true for as long as the brine
   stays wet. Every use takes one memory of a taste from the writer, forever. It cannot be used
   on the dead, and it cannot make anyone love anyone.
4. **No horses.** There is no room for horses in Saltmarrow. Goods move by rope-lift, handcart
   and porter. A horse in the city would be a sensation.
5. **Money.** Copper bits, silver ribs and the rare gold vertebra. Ten bits to a rib, twenty
   ribs to a vertebra.
6. **The sea is never called the sea** inside the city. It is "the Grey" or "the water".

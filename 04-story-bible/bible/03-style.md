# Saltmarrow story bible: style guide

- **Tense and person:** present tense, close third person, always from Ilse's point of view.
  The narration never knows what Ilse does not.
- **Sentences:** short and concrete. Long sentences only for the water.
- **Senses:** every scene has salt in it somewhere: a taste, a crust, a smell, a sting.
- **Dialogue:** sparse. People in Saltmarrow do not explain what both of them already know.
  No dialogue tags fancier than "says".
- **Words we never use:** "suddenly", "magic", "okay", "the sea" (inside the city).
- **Time:** measured in bells and tides, never in minutes or hours, except the Low Hour.
- **Endings:** a scene ends on an image, not on a line of dialogue.
- **Tone:** quiet dread, small warmth. Nobody is a villain; everybody wants something.

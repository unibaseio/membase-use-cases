# Scene 1: The compass

The Gullet smells of tallow and wet rope. Ilse buys kelp from the woman with the cracked
ladle and pays in copper bits, four of them, warm from her pocket.

Pell finds her at the third stall. "Eleven gulls on the skull," Pell says. "Twelve. The warden
wants you."

"Hm."

Tobiah Fenn is waiting in his watch-house in the left eye socket, whistling the first four bars
of "The Drowned Clockmaker" and then stopping, and then starting again. His grey eye finds Ilse
before his brown one does.

"Cartographer," he says. "The Abbess asked about your brother's compass."

Ilse puts her hand on her coat pocket. The brass is cold through the oilskin. She does not need
to look. She knows where the needle points: up the Lantern Stair, through four hundred and
twelve steps of bone, to the red room under the Spine Chapel.

"She can ask," Ilse says.

Outside, the first tide-bell rings. On the fourth Rib Bridge the rope barrier sways in the wind,
and a crust of salt flakes off it and falls into the Grey.

# Saltmarrow story bible: characters

## Ilse Varrow (protagonist, point-of-view character)

- Thirty-four. Was a ropewright on the Lower Ribs for twelve years; now the city's only
  licensed tide-cartographer, which means she maps where the water will be, not where the
  streets are.
- Left-handed. Writes her maps in brown iron-gall ink and never in black, because black ink
  "looks like a drowned thing".
- Carries her dead brother Corin's brass compass. It has never pointed north. Its needle points
  to the leviathan's heart, under the Spine Chapel, wherever in the city she stands.
- Has used salt-script three times. Each use cost her the memory of a taste: cinnamon, plums
  and her mother's barley bread. She can still eat them; they taste of nothing.
- Does not drink wine or ale. Drinks bitter kelp tea, black, from a dented tin cup.
- Speaks in short sentences. Says "Hm" instead of yes. Never swears in front of Pell.
- Wears a sea-green oilskin coat with one brass button missing (the third from the top).
- Fear: the Low Hour. Corin drowned on a Rib Bridge during it, eleven years ago.

## Tobiah Fenn (harbor-warden)

- Fifty-six. Warden of the Gullet Harbor. Heavy, slow, kind, and far cleverer than he lets on.
- Mismatched eyes: the left one grey, the right one brown.
- Cannot swim and is afraid of deep water, which everyone in the harbor knows and nobody
  mentions.
- Whistles the old shanty "The Drowned Clockmaker" when he is nervous. Only the first four bars;
  he has never learned the rest.
- Owes Ilse a debt he will not name. Calls her "Cartographer", never "Ilse".

## Pell (messenger)

- Twelve. An orphan who runs messages between the tiers for copper bits.
- Counts everything aloud: steps, gulls, bells, coins. When frightened, counts backwards.
- Barefoot in every season; her soles are hard as horn.
- Believes the leviathan is only sleeping. Leaves a pinch of salt on the Spine Chapel step
  every morning "so it does not wake hungry".

## Mother Odile (Marrow Abbess)

- Head of the Order of the Spine, which keeps the Spine Chapel and rings the tide-bells.
- Seventy, tall, blind in both eyes since birth. Reads salt-script by touch, which no one else
  can do.
- Wants Ilse's compass. Has never said so aloud.
- Smells of tallow and cloves. Her staff is a single rib of a gull, bound in silver.

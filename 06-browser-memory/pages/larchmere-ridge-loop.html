TRIP REPORT · LARCHMERE VALLEY

Larchmere Ridge Loop: trail notes

The Larchmere Ridge Loop is 14.2 km with 860 m of climbing, run clockwise from the Holloway trailhead. Most people do it in six to seven hours.

Water

There is exactly one reliable water source on the whole loop: Tamsin Spring, at km 9, just below Owl Saddle. The creek near the start is dry by midsummer. Carry at least 2 litres from the trailhead and refill at Tamsin Spring; filter it, there are cattle upstream in spring.

Rules and timing

  • The Holloway car-park gate is locked at 18:00 sharp. Rangers do not unlock it.
  • No dogs past Owl Saddle (ground-nesting curlews, March to August).
  • The ridge section between km 5 and km 8 is fully exposed: no shelter at all in rain or lightning.

Verdict

Gorgeous on a clear day. Miserable and slippery on the ridge when it is wet; the granite slabs at km 6 are the worst part.

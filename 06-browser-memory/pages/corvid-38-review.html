Field test: the Corvid 38 daypack

The Corvid 38 from Rookery Outdoor weighs 1.1 kg and fits torso lengths from 41 to 48 cm. We carried it for three weeks.

What we liked

  • The hip belt pockets take a phone and a snack bar each.
  • Side pockets fit a 1 litre bottle securely. A 1.5 litre bottle falls out on the first scramble.
  • The back panel breathes well on hot climbs.

What we did not

  • No rain cover is included, and the fabric is not waterproof: after an hour of steady rain everything in the main compartment was damp. Buy the separate Corvid rain cover (sold on its own) or pack a liner.
  • The lid pocket zip sticks when the pack is full.

Verdict: a great fair-weather day pack; budget for the rain cover.
